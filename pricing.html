Questions & Answers

What's the difference between Guest Mode and Standard?

Guest Mode runs entirely on your own hardware - no account, no cloud, no quota. Standard adds VEIL Cloud access, unlimited local usage, document ingestion, and report export. Same engine either way; Standard just removes the ceiling.

Do I need a credit card to try Oriphim?

No. Guest Mode asks for nothing - no card, no sign-up. You only enter billing details if you choose Standard or Enterprise.

What counts against the VEIL Cloud quota?

Only cloud completions - each one is counted server-side as it happens. Local chat and local simulation runs never touch the quota, since they run on your machine, not ours.

Can I cancel or switch billing anytime?

Standard is month-to-month and cancels anytime. Annual billing is prepaid for the year at the discounted rate. Enterprise self-serve bills monthly by default; annual contracts are available on request.

Is the Enterprise roadmap list a real commitment?

Yes, but it's not live yet - that's why it's labeled separately. SSO, multi-seat accounts, an admin console, and CI integration are committed and in progress, not shipped today. Enterprise partners get priority input on the order we build them in.

Is Oriphim actually private and offline-capable?

Yes - that's the architecture, not a feature flag. Oriphim runs fully local by default. Enterprise adds a signed data-residency attestation and network-egress audit doc on top of that existing fact, for procurement review.
