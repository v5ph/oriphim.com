Our mission

Lower the cost of scientific discovery. Starting from a paper or research idea, Oriphim recreates and runs computational experiments conversationally, letting researchers verify findings, explore work they're curious about, and make genuine discoveries. Without expertise barriers, expensive infrastructure, or prohibitive time investment.

Evidence

Verification and Validation: Two tiers we don't conflate.

Verification asks: Is the model mathematically correct? We check convergence under refinement, conservation laws, integrator agreement, and whether conclusions hold across every free parameter. Verification requires no real-world data, so it's available for any system you can describe.

Validation asks: How does the model compare to reality? We carry uncertainty on both sides. Validation requires real data and is earned one domain at a time.

Oriphim labels every report with its tier. We don't claim your model is correct. We claim it was solved to a stated accuracy, or that it diverges from reality by a quantified amount under stated conditions. Both findings are defensible. Both matter.

How We Work

Five commitments that shape everything we build.

I

Run it, don't cite it.

Claims become code. Code gets executed. You learn faster by simulating something and being wrong than by citing it and never checking.

II

Local-first, always.

Your research runs on your hardware by default. Cloud is optional, never hidden. You own your data and experiments.

III

Critique is our job.

A plausible but silent error is the failure mode we engineer against. Finding those errors is the product itself, not something left to expert users.

IV

Judgment stays human.

Oriphim produces the model, the run, the checks, and the reasoning in hours instead of months. A named researcher reviews, corrects, and owns every result. Every change is tracked.

V

Shipped and roadmap are labeled.

You always know what's real today and what's coming. No hidden assumptions.
