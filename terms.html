Privacy

The short version: experiments run on your computer, we collect little, we don’t sell data or show ads, and the site has no analytics or tracking cookies.

When you use Ori

  • Your experiments run on your computer. Code, simulations and results are written to a folder on your machine. We don’t run them on our servers.
  • Ori thinks with Claude. To plan, write code and review results, Ori sends what it’s working on to Anthropic using your key: your messages, and the parts of your papers, notes, code and data it reads. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, this isn’t used to train their models, and Anthropic keeps it for up to 30 days to detect misuse. Nothing passes through us.
  • Web lookups. When Ori looks up a method, constant or reference result, its search terms leave your computer and can describe what you’re working on. For a sensitive project, tell Ori not to search.
  • No telemetry. Ori sends us no usage or research data. Diagnostics (ori doctor) leave your computer only if you send them to us.
  • Installing and updating. The installer and ori update download from oriphim.com and from GitHub, which see the request as for any download.

When you use our site

  • Accounts. If you create one, we keep your email address and how you signed in (Google, GitHub or an emailed code), through Supabase, our database provider. If you join our list, we keep that choice and email you updates; you can leave at any time.
  • The contact form. What you write (your name, email, company and what you’re working on) is emailed to us through Resend. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you’re a person, not a bot.
  • Payments. Stripe handles payments. We never see your card number; we keep invoices and billing records.
  • Hosting. Cloudflare serves the site and processes standard request data, such as your IP address and browser, to deliver and protect it. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google.
  • Browser storage. Signing in keeps your session in your browser’s storage. Turnstile may set a cookie it needs for bot protection. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

When you work with us

For a Groundwork, we run the work on our own machine from files you share with us. We use your files only for that work and delete them within 30 days of delivery, unless you ask us to keep them. We’ll sign an NDA if you want one.

Who we share data with

Only the providers that run our services (Supabase, Cloudflare, Resend, Stripe and Google Fonts, as described above), and anyone the law requires us to. We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t use it to train AI models. Some of these providers are in the United States, so your data may be processed there.

How long we keep it

Account data stays while your account exists, and is deleted when you ask us to close it. Contact messages are kept [for up to two years] so we can follow up. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.
