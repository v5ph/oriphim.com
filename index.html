Method

How a claim becomes a result.

Three passes, the same for every domain - so the output can always be traced back to the source.

01
Ingest
Upload the paper, dataset, or stated problem. Oriphim reads it and pulls out the claims that can be tested.
02
Derive
Each claim becomes a formal spec - equations, constraints, a model - ready to run rather than just read.
03
Simulate
The spec runs as a real simulation, checked against conservation laws, known solutions, or counterexample search.
